**Short answer:** A Dallas–Fort Worth location qualifies for a free vending machine from Texas Vending when it has at least 50 people in total foot traffic. That number counts employees and visitors together. At a qualifying site, the equipment, delivery, setup, and service cost you nothing, and there is no long-term contract.

Search "free vending machine requirements" and you will find a different number on almost every vendor's site. Some count only employees. Some ask for daily visitors. Some don't say at all. This guide explains our rule, shows how to count your site in about five minutes, and lists what "free" covers.

## What Is the 50-Person Rule?

The rule is one total: **employees plus visitors, at least 50 people.** It is not 50 employees, and it is not 50 visitors. A small office with steady client traffic can qualify. So can a warehouse with drivers and contractors coming through.

The threshold exists because a vending company pays for the machine, the delivery, the setup, and the service, and earns its money from product sales. A location needs enough people buying to cover that work. Fifty people in total foot traffic is the point where we can install and service equipment at no cost to you.

## Who Counts Toward the 50?

Count everyone who uses the location and could buy from the machine:

- **Employees.** Full-time and part-time staff who work on site.
- **Customers and clients.** People who come to your counter, showroom, or lobby.
- **Patients and their families.** For clinics, medical offices, and waiting rooms.
- **Drivers.** Truck drivers waiting on a dock or a load.
- **Contractors and temps.** Crews and agency workers who are on site regularly.
- **Students and members.** For schools, training centers, and gyms.

Add them into one number. If you are close to 50, include it in your request. We confirm the count on the site walk.

## Three Example Counts

| Location | Employees | Visitors | Total | Qualifies? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Law office in Uptown Dallas | 30 | 25 clients | 55 | Yes |
| Distribution center in south Fort Worth | 40 | 15 drivers and contractors | 55 | Yes |
| Small design studio | 20 | A few visitors | About 25 | Below the threshold |

The law office would not qualify on employees alone. Its client traffic carries it over 50. The design studio is below the line, so it should talk to us before expecting free placement.

## What You Provide

At a qualifying location, your part is simple:

1. **Floor space** for the machine, smart cooler, or market.
2. **A power outlet** near that space.
3. **Access** for our team to stock and service the equipment. We plan visits around your shifts, docks, and badge rules.

That's it. You don't buy the equipment, you don't stock it, and you don't handle repairs.

## What Is Free, and What Is Not?

Free placement covers the full vending program at a qualifying location:

- **Equipment, delivery, and setup:** $0. No installation fee.
- **Service and restocking:** no service fee. Real-time monitoring tells us when a machine needs service, and we set a restocking schedule for your location.
- **Repairs:** local technicians respond to reported issues within 24 hours, including after hours.
- **Contract:** no long-term contract required.
- **Payments:** every machine, micro market, and coffee system is cashless. Cards, mobile wallets, and tap-to-pay all work.

One exception to know up front: **office coffee and water.** The coffee machine and water machine installation is free, but coffee, tea, water, and supplies are billed. See [coffee and water service](/our-services/coffee-water/) for details.

Fifty people qualifies you for free placement. The right equipment depends on your space, your hours, and what your team wants to eat. The main options:

## How to Check If Your Site Qualifies

1. **Count your employees** who work on site.
2. **Estimate your regular visitors:** customers, patients, drivers, contractors.
3. **Add the two.** If the total is 50 or more, you likely qualify.
4. **Note your space and outlet.** A rough location is enough for now.
5. **[Send the details](/contact-us/).** The contact form asks for your foot traffic range. We then walk the site and confirm.
