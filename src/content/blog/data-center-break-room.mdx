The best fit is usually an AI smart cooler with fresh meals and drinks, plus a coffee machine, serviced on a schedule that respects your badge rules. The people inside them, including technicians, security officers, and contractors on build-outs, work long shifts behind controlled doors. Food access is a real operational issue, not a perk.

## Why Is Food Access Hard at a Data Center?

- **Leaving takes time.** Every exit and re-entry goes through badge checks, mantraps, or escorts.
- **Shifts run all night.** Restaurants close. Delivery to a secure door is slow or not allowed.
- **Space is tight.** The break room is often a small room, sized after the white space was planned.
- **Few people, high stakes.** A small overnight team cannot all leave at once.

## How Do Service Visits Work at a Secure Site?

Security teams worry about outside vendors. A good service plan keeps visits few, short, and predictable:

1. **Pre-cleared people.** Ask your vendor to register its route staff with your security team in advance.
2. **Planned windows.** We plan every service visit around your badge rules and shift schedule.
3. **Fewer trips.** Equipment reports sales in real time, so each visit is planned in advance and focused on what is running low, not a walk-through to check.
4. **Fast fixes.** If something fails, local technicians respond to reported issues within 24 hours, including after hours.
