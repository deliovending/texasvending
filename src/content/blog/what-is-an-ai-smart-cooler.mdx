**Short answer:** An AI smart cooler is a self-service, glass-door fridge for the workplace. You tap a card or phone to unlock it, take what you want, and close the door. The cooler detects what you took and charges you automatically. It holds fresh meals, sandwiches, salads, drinks, and snacks in about the floor space of a refrigerator, and it works around the clock with no cashier.

Smart coolers are showing up in office kitchens, warehouse break rooms, hospitals, and lobbies across Dallas–Fort Worth. Employees love them because they feel like opening a fridge at home. Facilities teams like them because they fit where a full market will not.

## How Does a Smart Cooler Work?

It takes about as long as grabbing a drink from your own refrigerator:

1. **Tap to unlock.** Hold a credit or debit card, a phone, or a watch to the reader. The door unlocks.
2. **Take what you want.** Pick up items, read labels, and change your mind. Put something back before you close the door and you are not charged for it.
3. **Close the door.** The cooler detects what was taken and charges your card automatically.

No scanning, no keypad, and no "item stuck in the spiral."

## What Makes It "AI"?

The "AI" refers to how the cooler knows what you took. Smart coolers use technology such as cameras with image recognition, weight sensors on the shelves, or both, to track which items leave the cooler. That is what lets the door stay open for browsing while the cooler still charges correctly. It also gives the vending company real-time sales data, so restocking follows what people actually buy.

## What Can a Smart Cooler Hold?

Far more than a coil vending machine:

- **Fresh meals:** sandwiches, wraps, salads, and ready-to-eat meals.
- **Yogurt, fruit cups, and protein snacks.**
- **Cold drinks:** water, sodas, juices, energy drinks, and coffee drinks.
- **Snacks** on dry shelves.

Because nothing has to fit in a spiral, odd-shaped packages and glass bottles are no problem. See our [fresh food](/fresh-food/) menu for examples.

## Smart Cooler, Micro Market, or Vending Machine?

| | Smart cooler | Micro market | Vending machine |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Footprint** | About one refrigerator | A wall or a small room | One machine |
| **Fresh food** | Yes | Yes, the most variety | Limited |
| **How you pay** | Tap to unlock, charged on close | Scan and pay at a kiosk | Pay, then the item drops |
| **Best for** | Small or secured spaces | Larger break rooms | Snacks and drinks only |

We recommend the right fit after a site walk, not before. Read [what is a micro market](/blog/what-is-a-micro-market/) for the larger option.
