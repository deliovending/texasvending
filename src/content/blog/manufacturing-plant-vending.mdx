**Short answer:** A plant break room has to feed several shifts, in short breaks, often far from the line. Most North Texas plants do best with fresh food in the main break room, from a micro market or AI smart coolers, plus snack and drink machines closer to the floor. Everything should be cashless and serviced around production. Texas Vending places this equipment at no cost at plants with at least 50 people in total foot traffic.

Plant managers ask sharper questions than most buyers. They run schedules to the minute, answer to safety and quality teams, and have seen vendors overpromise. Here are the questions we hear most, and straight answers.

## How Far Is the Break Room From the Line?

This is the first question, because it decides how much of a break is actually rest. In a large plant, a five-minute walk each way turns a 15-minute break into five minutes of sitting down.

Options that help:

- **A second food point.** Snack and drink machines or a smart cooler near the far end of the floor.
- **Fresh food where people already eat**, so lunch does not require a drive.
- **Fast checkout.** A smart cooler or a micro market kiosk moves a shift change through in minutes.

## How Do Service Visits Fit Production?

Vendors who show up during a shift change and block the break room do not last. A good plan:

- **Visits around your schedule.** We plan service visits around your shifts, docks, and badge rules.
- **Data, not guesswork.** Equipment reports sales in real time, so each visit restocks what is actually running low.
- **Fast repairs.** Local technicians respond to reported issues within 24 hours, including after hours, so a 3rd shift problem does not wait for Monday.

## What About Food Safety and Quality?

Quality teams will ask where fresh food comes from and how it is kept cold. Ask any vendor:

1. How is fresh food dated, and who pulls expired items?
2. What happens to food in a cooler if the power fails?
3. Is packaging labeled and tamper evident?

Texas sets rules for unstaffed markets that cover these points. Our post on [Texas micro market rules](/blog/texas-micro-market-rules/) explains them.

## What Does It Cost the Plant?

At a location with at least 50 people in total foot traffic, counting employees and visitors such as drivers and contractors, the equipment, delivery, setup, and service cost nothing. There is no installation fee, no service fee, and no long-term contract. Coffee and water supplies are the one billed item.
