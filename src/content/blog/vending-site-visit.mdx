**Short answer:** A vending site visit is a short walk through your building before any equipment is recommended. We look at where people take breaks, how they move between shifts, the space and outlets available, and your access rules. We also confirm your total foot traffic, employees and visitors combined. You get a recommendation that fits the site, and the visit is free.

Some vendors quote from a phone call and drop off a machine that does not fit the room, the outlet, or the team. Texas Vending walks every site before quoting. Here is what that walk covers, so you know what to expect and what to have ready.

## What Do We Look At?

### Where people take breaks

The official break room is not always where people actually go. We look at break rooms, lounges, kitchens, and the spots near time clocks, docks, and restrooms where people gather.

### How far people walk

On a large floor, a long walk eats the break. We note the distance from work areas to each possible spot and whether a second food point would help.

### Space, power, and access

For each spot we check:

- Floor space, plus room in front to stand, pay, and open doors
- A nearby outlet, without extension cords
- Air around refrigerated equipment
- Doors, hallways, and elevators on the path from the dock
- Clear floor space and an accessible route to the equipment

### Your schedule and rules

Service visits are planned around your shifts, docks, and badge rules, so we ask about them on the walk: check-in steps, escort needs, dock hours, and times to avoid.

## What Questions Do We Ask?

- **How many people are on site, by shift?** Employees and regular visitors count together. A location qualifies for free placement with at least 50 people in total foot traffic.
- **What do people eat now?** Drive-thru runs, a snack drawer, a cafeteria that closes at 2 p.m.?
- **Who works nights and weekends?**
- **What did not work before?** Empty machines, slow repairs, or a contract that was hard to leave.
- **Do you want coffee and water?** Machine installation is free; coffee, tea, water, and supplies are billed.

## What Happens After the Visit?

1. **We recommend a setup:** [vending machines](/our-services/vending-machine/), [AI smart coolers](/our-services/ai-smart-cooler/), a [micro market](/our-services/micro-markets/), coffee service, or a mix.
2. **We confirm whether the site qualifies** for free placement, and say so plainly if it does not.
3. **You review the terms.** No long-term contract is required. Our [vending contract checklist](/blog/vending-contract-checklist/) lists what to ask any vendor.
4. **We install, stock, and service the equipment.** Real-time monitoring tells us when a machine needs service, and we set a restocking schedule for your location.
