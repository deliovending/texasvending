## What to Look for in a Reliable Vending Service

Use the themes above as a checklist for any vendor, including us. Each one comes with a question to ask before you sign.

### 1. Follow-through

Hanna’s first word of praise is “follow through.” A vending company makes promises during the sale: the setup, the products, the schedule. The test is whether those promises still hold in month six.

**Ask:** “Can I talk to a customer whose site looks like mine?” An office and a three-shift warehouse need different things, so ask for a reference that matches.

### 2. A real path for product requests

Your team knows what it wants to buy. A good vendor gives you a simple way to ask for it and tells you what happens next. Texas Vending adds specific products and brands when there is enough demand, and sales data shows what actually sells. Our [vending drink catalog](/beverages/) shows the kind of lineup we start from.

**Ask:** “How do we request a product, and how do we find out whether it was added?”

### 3. Restocking that keeps up with demand

Daniel and James both point to restocking. Empty spirals are the most visible sign of a vendor falling behind, and a fixed calendar alone can leave slots empty between visits.

Every machine breaks eventually. What matters is how fast someone shows up. Texas Vending technicians respond to reported service issues within 24 hours, including after hours, and our [vending repair service](/our-services/vending-repair/) covers machines no matter who supplied them.

## How Do You Judge Vending Company Reviews?

Reviews and testimonials help, but read them like a facilities manager, not a shopper:

- **Look for specifics.** “Great company” says little. “Keeps up with demand” or “updates us on implementation progress” describes real work.
- **Match the site type.** Praise from an office tells you less about a 24-hour warehouse, and the reverse is also true.
- **Check the terms behind the praise.** A friendly vendor can still have an auto-renewal clause and a removal fee. Our [vending contract checklist](/blog/vending-contract-checklist/) lists the ten questions to settle before you sign.
- **See how they start.** A vendor that quotes without seeing your building is guessing. Here is [what happens on a vending site visit](/blog/vending-site-visit/) with us.
