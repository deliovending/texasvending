## Which Workplaces Need 24-Hour Snack Options?

Any site where people work, wait, or visit after normal hours. Some common ones in DFW:

- **Hospitals and clinics.** Nurses, techs, and physicians work around the clock, and families sit in waiting rooms long after the cafeteria closes. See [hospital vending](/industries-we-serve/hospitals/).
- **Data centers.** Small overnight teams work behind badge doors, often far from any open restaurant. See [data center vending](/industries-we-serve/data-center/).
- **24-hour gyms.** Members train before sunrise and after midnight and want a protein snack or a cold drink on the way out. See [fitness center vending](/industries-we-serve/fitness/).
- **Hotels and front desks.** Night staff can't leave the desk, and guests want something at odd hours too.
- **Call centers.** Shifts that cover several time zones run late into the night.
- **Security desks.** One or two guards hold a post for an entire shift.
- **Offices where people stay late.** Deadlines, quarter closes, and year-end crunches keep people at their desks past dinner.

## Which 24-Hour Snack Options for Workplaces Really Stay Open?

A snack option is only 24-hour if it works without a person behind it. Three kinds of equipment do.

### Vending machines

Snack, drink, combo, and refrigerated [vending machines](/our-services/vending-machine/) run all night. Every Texas Vending machine takes cards, mobile wallets, and tap-to-pay, so nobody needs cash at 3 a.m.

### AI smart coolers

An [AI smart cooler](/our-services/ai-smart-cooler/) is a refrigerated case that unlocks with a tap. People open the door, take what they want, and get charged automatically. It holds items a spiral machine can't, such as yogurt, fruit, salads, and bottled drinks, and it fits in a small staff room, a lobby, or a gym corner.

### Micro markets

A [micro market](/our-services/micro-markets/) is a self-checkout store with open shelves and coolers. It is open around the clock and carries the widest selection, which suits larger round-the-clock sites such as hospitals.

What doesn't count as 24/7: a cafeteria that closes at 7, a cash honor box, or a snack drawer that depends on one person remembering to refill it.

## What Are the Best Vending Snacks for Night Shift Employees?

Late hours change what people need from a snack. NIOSH training for night-shift nurses recommends foods such as vegetables, salads, fruit, whole-grain sandwiches, yogurt, cheese, eggs, and nuts during the shift. It also suggests avoiding sugar-rich products and low-fiber carbohydrate foods, which can add to sleepiness when people need to stay alert.

A practical late-hours list:

- **Protein snacks:** nuts, trail mix, jerky, protein bars, and cheese-and-cracker packs.
- **From the cooler:** yogurt and parfaits, fruit cups, cheese, salads, and wraps.
- **Whole grains:** whole-grain crackers and sandwiches on whole-grain bread.
- **Low-sugar drinks:** water, sparkling water, and zero-sugar sodas and sports drinks.
- **Caffeine, with the label in view:** coffee, tea, and energy drinks. NIOSH suggests sticking to light caffeine during the shift. The FDA cites 400 milligrams a day as an amount not generally associated with negative effects for most adults, and it reports that energy drinks range from 54 to 328 milligrams of caffeine per 16 ounces. Our [beverage catalog](/beverages/) lists the caffeine in each drink we stock most often.
- **A few treats.** People still want chips and chocolate at midnight. The goal is choice, not a lecture.

## Where Should After-Hours Snacks Go?

A 24-hour machine behind a door that locks at 6 p.m. is a day-shift machine. When you pick the spot, think about the people who work at night:

- **Inside their badge zone.** Night staff shouldn't need an escort or a second key to reach food.
- **Close to the work.** A security guard or front desk clerk can't walk across a campus on a break. A small cooler near the post beats a big market three floors away.
- **Well lit and easy to find.** Some buildings dim hallways overnight, so pick a spot that stays bright.

Our guide on [where to put a vending machine](/blog/where-to-put-a-vending-machine/) covers power, space, and traffic in more detail.
