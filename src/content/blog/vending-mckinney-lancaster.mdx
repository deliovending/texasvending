**Short answer:** Texas Vending provides vending services in Lancaster, TX and McKinney with the same program at both ends of Dallas–Fort Worth: free placement at locations with at least 50 people in total foot traffic, cashless equipment, real-time monitoring, and no long-term contract. What changes is the setup. Lancaster's distribution centers usually need food near every break area across a big building. Lancaster sits on the south side of Dallas County, where I-20 meets I-35E. McKinney is the Collin County seat on the north side of the metro. ## What Do Vending Services in Lancaster, TX Look Like?

Lancaster's workplaces lean toward logistics. I-35E forms the city's western boundary, and Park 20/35, the largest logistics park in Lancaster, sits at I-20 and I-35E. Its tenants include names such as Quaker Oats/PepsiCo, Mars Petcare, and BMW. The city also runs Lancaster Regional Airport, a general aviation airport that serves as a reliever for DFW and Love Field.

That mix shapes the break room:

- **Big buildings.** A long walk to one break room eats a short break. Food close to each break area matters.
- **Shifts and docks.** Crews work days, nights, and weekends. Service visits have to work around dock schedules and gate or badge rules.
- **Peak season.** Extra shifts and new hires mean more people buying, so restocking has to keep up.

Lancaster is part of the southern Dallas County and northern Ellis County area on our [DeSoto, Lancaster, and south Dallas County page](/about-us/cities/desoto/), along with DeSoto, Duncanville, Red Oak, and Ferris.

## How Is McKinney Different?

McKinney mixes manufacturing, corporate offices, and county government. Raytheon is the city's largest employer. Encore Wire runs a single-site wire manufacturing campus here, alongside Globe Life, Simpson Strong-Tie, and Collin County.

The work spreads across three kinds of places:

- **Offices at Craig Ranch.** Office development centers on the McKinney Corporate Center at Craig Ranch, near SH-121. Offices there and around the historic downtown square tend to need a smaller setup.
- **Industry near the airport.** New industrial buildings are going up around McKinney National Airport in the southeast part of the city, including the McKinney Air Business Park.
- **Round-the-clock and public sites.** Medical City McKinney and Baylor Scott & White McKinney staff around the clock, and county offices see a steady flow of public visitors.

## What Setup Fits Each Kind of Site?

The city matters less than the building. Here is what tends to fit, whether the site is in Lancaster, McKinney, or anywhere between. We confirm the details on a site walk before recommending anything.

### Distribution centers and warehouses

Common in Lancaster, and in the newer industrial buildings near McKinney's airport. A typical setup pairs an [AI smart cooler](/our-services/ai-smart-cooler/) or a [micro market](/our-services/micro-markets/) with fresh meals in the main break room and snack and drink [vending machines](/our-services/vending-machine/) closer to the docks. Everything works around the clock without a cashier, so night crews get the same food as day shift. Our [logistics and distribution page](/industries-we-serve/logistics/) covers this setup in more depth.

### Manufacturing plants

McKinney has plants such as the Encore Wire campus, and plant shifts are common across the south side of the metro too. Plants run shifts and have gates and docks that service visits must work around. Short breaks favor food close to the floor. A smart cooler or a cooler wall with a kiosk feeds a shift change without a line. See [manufacturing facility vending](/industries-we-serve/manufacturing-facilities/) for more.

### Offices

Craig Ranch and downtown McKinney offices often fit a smaller setup: coffee service plus a smart cooler for fresh sandwiches, salads, and meals on one floor. Larger office floors can support a micro market. Coffee machine installation is free; coffee, tea, and supplies are billed.

### Hospitals and public buildings

Round-the-clock staff need food at 3 a.m., and public visitors need something in the waiting area. Cashless machines and smart coolers cover both without a cashier.
