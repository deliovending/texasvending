**Short answer:** Put the machine where people already walk on their break, close to where they work. The best spots are the break room, a hallway near time clocks or restrooms, or a lounge. The spot needs floor space, a nearby outlet, room to stand and pay, and air around refrigerated machines. Avoid dead-end corners and anywhere a long walk eats the break.

Placement decides whether a machine sells or sits. The same equipment can sell far more a few steps from where it started, simply because more people pass it. Here is how to pick the spot.

## Rule 1: Follow the Traffic

People buy what they pass. Map how your team moves during a shift:

- **Entrances and time clocks**, where every shift starts and ends.
- **Restrooms and water fountains**, which everyone visits.
- **The break room or lounge**, where people already sit down.

A machine on that path gets seen several times a day. A machine behind a closed door gets forgotten.

## Rule 2: Keep the Walk Short

On a large floor, the walk is part of the break. If the break room is a five-minute walk from the line or the dock, people skip it. Consider:

- **A second food point** closer to the far end of the floor.
- **Smaller equipment in more places**, such as a smart cooler near the dock and a market in the main break room.

## Rule 3: Check Power and Space

Every spot needs:

1. **A nearby outlet.** Avoid extension cords and power strips.
2. **Floor space** for the machine, plus room in front for people to stand, pay, and open doors.
3. **Air around refrigerated machines** so they can vent heat. The manual lists the clearance.
4. **A level, dry floor**, away from mop sinks and dock doors that let in rain.

## Placement Mistakes to Avoid

- **The empty corner.** Out of sight, out of mind.
- **The locked room.** If night shift cannot reach it, it does not serve night shift.
- **Blocking a path.** Machines in narrow halls create jams at shift change.
- **Direct sun.** Refrigerated machines work harder and fail sooner.
- **Next to the microwave line.** It crowds both.
