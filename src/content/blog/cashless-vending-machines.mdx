**Short answer:** A cashless vending machine has a card reader that accepts credit and debit cards, mobile wallets on a phone or watch, and tap-to-pay cards. You pay first, then choose an item, or with a smart cooler, you tap to unlock and are charged when you close the door. Every Texas Vending machine, micro market, and coffee system is cashless.

Most people do not carry cash to work anymore, and a machine that only takes dollar bills is a machine that does not sell. Here is how each type of cashless payment works, and what to do when one fails.

## How Do You Pay at a Cashless Machine?

### Tap a card or phone

Hold a contactless card, phone, or watch near the reader. It is the fastest option and works with Apple Pay, Google Pay, and similar wallets.

### Insert or swipe a card

For cards without tap, insert the chip or swipe at the reader.

## How Is Paying Different at Each Type of Equipment?

- **Vending machine:** pay at the reader, choose your selection, and the item drops.
- **AI smart cooler:** tap to unlock the door, take what you want, close the door. The cooler detects what you took and charges you automatically. [How smart coolers work](/blog/what-is-an-ai-smart-cooler/).
- **Micro market:** scan each item at the self-checkout kiosk, then tap or insert to pay. [What is a micro market?](/blog/what-is-a-micro-market/)
- **Coffee machines:** pay at the reader, then brew.

## What If a Cashless Payment Fails?

Most failures have simple causes:

1. **The card was not held long enough.** Hold it near the reader until it beeps or shows a confirmation.
2. **The reader lost its connection.** After a power blip, readers can take a few minutes to reconnect.
3. **The card was declined by the bank.** Try another card or a phone wallet.

If the machine charged you and did not vend, note the location, the time, and the last four digits of the card, and contact the vending company. Cashless sales leave a record, which makes refunds simple to trace. If a reader keeps failing, it needs service. See [vending machine repair in Dallas](/blog/vending-machine-repair-dallas/).

## Why Are Businesses Going Cashless?

For the host business, cashless equipment removes a list of small problems:

- **No cash on site** to collect, count, or guard.
- **No change runs** and no jammed bill validators.
- **More sales**, because people can buy with what is already in their pocket.
- **Real-time data.** Each sale is recorded, so the vending company sees what sells and restocks to match.
