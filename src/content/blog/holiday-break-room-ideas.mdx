**Short answer:** The easiest holiday upgrade is food people use every day. Add seasonal snacks and drinks, keep the coffee station full, set up a shared treat table, and plan the break room around peak-season shifts and holiday closures. Tell your vending company early so stocking matches the calendar.

November and December are busy everywhere in Dallas–Fort Worth. Offices push year-end deadlines. Warehouses and distribution centers run peak season with extra shifts and new faces. The break room is where people catch their breath, and a few small changes make it feel like the season.

## 1. Bring In Seasonal Snacks and Drinks

A few seasonal items go further than a new poster:

- Holiday flavors of familiar snacks and coffee drinks
- Hot cocoa and tea next to the coffee
- Festive treats for the shared table, from bakery cookies to candy

Ask your vending company what it can add. Texas Vending adds specific products and brands when there is enough demand, and sales data shows what sells.

## 2. Keep the Coffee Flowing

Long days at year end run on coffee. Make sure the station keeps up:

- Check that cups, lids, creamer, and sweeteners are stocked before busy weeks.
- Add a second brew option if lines form in the morning.
- Leave room for hot water and tea for people who skip coffee.

## 3. Plan for Peak Season Shifts

Warehouses and plants often add shifts and temporary staff in the last weeks of the year. More people on site means:

- **More traffic** at every break, especially at shift change.
- **Night shifts** that need real food after the cafeteria closes. See [night shift food](/blog/night-shift-break-room-food/).
- **A higher total count** of people on site, which can make a site eligible for free placement for the first time. Our threshold is at least 50 people in total foot traffic, counting employees and visitors.

Tell your vending company your peak dates. Equipment reports sales in real time, so restocking can follow the extra demand.

## 4. Plan Around Closures

Holiday weeks are uneven. Some days are packed and some are nearly empty. To avoid waste and empty shelves:

- Share closure dates and skeleton-crew days with your vendor early.
- Let the vendor sell down fresh food before long closures.
- Make sure the people working through the holidays still have food. A smart cooler or micro market stays open around the clock.

## 5. Make a Break Everyone Can Join

Not everyone can make the evening party. A morning coffee break or an afternoon treat table during each shift reaches the night crew, the remote team on their in-office day, and the people covering phones.
