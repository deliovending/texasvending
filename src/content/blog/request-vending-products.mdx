## Can You Request Specific Snacks in Your Vending Machine?

Yes. A good vending company wants to know what your team will buy, because a product people want sells, and a product nobody wants sits. You can ask for:

- **Snacks:** a specific chip, bar, candy, jerky, or trail mix.
- **Drinks:** a soda, sparkling water, sports drink, energy drink, or bottled coffee. Our [vending drink catalog](/beverages/) lists common choices with sugar, caffeine, and allergens.
- **Fresh food:** sandwiches, salads, wraps, ready-to-eat meals, and breakfast. See our [fresh food](/fresh-food/) menu.
- **Coffee brands:** beans, creamers, and sweeteners for coffee service. Our [office coffee menu](/fresh-food/coffee-menu/) shows the lineup.

## How Do You Make a Vending Request?

A request with details gets results faster than “can we get better snacks?” Here is the simple version:

1. **Pick one point person.** An office, facilities, or HR manager collects requests so the vendor gets one clear list, not ten emails.
2. **Be specific.** Name the brand, flavor, and size. A photo of the package helps.
3. **Show the demand.** Note how many people asked and which shift they work. Ten people on second shift asking for the same item is a strong request.
4. **Send it to your vending company.** Use the contact your vendor gave you. Texas Vending customers can reach us through our [contact page](/contact-us/).
5. **Buy it when it shows up.** Sales data decides what stays. The best way to keep a requested item is for the people who asked to buy it.

## What Makes a Product Request Stick?

Two things decide whether a request becomes a permanent part of the lineup: demand and fit.

### Demand

Sales data shows what sells. If an item sells, it earns its slot. If it sits, the slot could hold something your team will actually buy. This is why one person’s favorite is harder to keep than a product a whole shift wants.

### Fit with your equipment

Some requests need different equipment, not just a different slot:

- **Shelf-stable snacks** fit a snack or combo machine.
- **Cold drinks** fit a drink machine, a cooler, or a market.
- **Fresh food** like sandwiches, salads, and meals needs refrigerated equipment built for it, such as an [AI smart cooler](/our-services/ai-smart-cooler/) or a [micro market](/our-services/micro-markets/).
- **Frozen items** like ice cream and frozen meals need a frozen machine. Our [equipment page](/equipment/) lists the options.
- **Coffee brands** come through [coffee and water service](/our-services/coffee-water/). Machine installation is free; coffee, tea, water, and supplies are billed.

## Custom Product Selection for Office Vending

There is no single right menu for every workplace. An office with a lot of meetings buys differently from a warehouse running three shifts. That is why the lineup should be set per location, not copied from a standard list.

At Texas Vending, it starts with a site visit. We walk your building, ask who works when, and recommend equipment and a starting mix. After install, real-time monitoring and sales data show what each location buys, and the restocking schedule is set for your site. Requests from your team fill in what the data cannot see yet: the new hire who wants a specific drink, or the crew asking for breakfast at the end of third shift.

## Can You Request Gluten-Free, Low-Sugar, or Other Dietary Options?

Yes. Dietary requests are some of the most common, and they often come from more than one person. Typical categories include:

- **Gluten-free** snacks and bars
- **Low-sugar and zero-sugar** drinks and snacks
- **High-protein** snacks and drinks for long, physical shifts
- **Vegetarian** meals and snacks
- **Lighter options** like nuts, fruit cups, and baked snacks

The same rules apply: be specific and show demand. One more rule matters here. The product label is the final word on ingredients and allergens. Anyone with a food allergy or a medical diet should read the label on every item before buying, even one they have bought before, because brands change recipes.
